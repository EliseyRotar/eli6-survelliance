Today is flag day, which is also the birthday of the United States Army.

On 14 June 1775 the Continental Congress adopted "The American Continental Army" after reaching a consensus position in The Committee of the Whole. Congress undertook to raise ten companies of riflemen, approved an enlistment form for them, and appointed a committee (including Washington and Schuyler) to draft rules and regulations for the government of the army. See: [1].

This makes the US Army is 230 years old. Strangely enough our army is one year and twenty days older than our country (4 July 1776). Rob, our resident U.S. Army (Retired) expert brought a flag for his door today.

23:46:19 Wednesday June 15 2005

So as it turns out I have a genetic disorder. Thanks mom and dad :) My dental hygienist discovered tori mandibularis in my mouth during my pre-cleaning screening. I didn't even notice them growing. I thought that everyone had them. Apparently this is not the case. It is an autosomal dominant trait most frequently found in populations which have historically lived in the northern latitudes. Interestingly it may be a cause of sleep apnea. Perhaps I should notify my father of this.
