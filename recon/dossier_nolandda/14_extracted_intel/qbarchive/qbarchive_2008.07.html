An Ash tree on Cumberland Avenue that is being used to test for the presence of Emerald Ash Borers (Agrilus planipennis). The USDA is conducting the survey. More info about the survey here.

02:44:21 Sunday July 06 2008

The weekend of the 21st was Chris Constantine's bachelor party. Terry put together quite an evening of activities in his role as Best Man.

We began with a fantastic dinner at Sullivans Steakhouse in Keystone.

Afterward we moved to Cracker's Comedy Club in Broad Ripple. There we enjoyed the comedy stylings of Dan Cummins and Jim Holder. I was surprised at the quality of their routines. I expected local talent that was very hit and miss, but they were quite good. We enjoyed it a lot. Were it not for the 2 drink minimum on top of the ticket price I might frequent Crackers more often.

The evening concluded at Brad's Gold Club, the strip club near Lafayette Square Mall. Needless to say, my photo set does not include anything from there. The place seemed surprisingly empty for a Saturday night, and the music selection could be charitably be described as dreadful. None the less the strippers were friendly and the bachelor got plenty of attention so our mission was accomplished.
