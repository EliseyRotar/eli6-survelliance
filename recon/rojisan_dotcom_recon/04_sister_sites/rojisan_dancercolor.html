About the widget ( New widget - Please report any strange behavior. Suggestions welcome! )

So, you're a belly dancer, are you? Sure, you've got a mad shimmy, and maybe you can balance a sabre on your nose, but there's so many different kinds of belly dancers out there in the wild world - Cabaret? Turkish? Lebanese? Tribal? Fusion? Tribal Carbaret Lebanese Egyptian Fusion on Stilts with Poi? It's all so confusing, how are you ever going to find your compatriots? With colors, of course! (hey, it works for gangs...)

We're here to help - find your color, show your color, and look for colors. Three core dimensions of belly dance boiled down to one 85x85 pixel square, and if you like we'll even put your name on it.
Enjoy!
