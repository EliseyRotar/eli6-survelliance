To those of you just joining us from the livejournal feed: welcome. I am Dan Noland, mild mannered computer hacker in West Lafayette, Indiana. I have other aliases on the network of course.

This weekend was quite busy. I was pleased to have dinner with my lovely and talented girlfriend, Sara with no 'h', on Friday after work. We skipped the Mardi Gras rush and waited until the first Friday of Lent to eat at the Cajun Connection.

On Saturday I attended Darwin Day on the IUPUI campus. The event was hosted by the Indiana branch of The Center for Inquiry. It featured a number of interesting speakers from Indiana Universities, Government, and Civil Liberties groups. There was even a debate between an Intelligent Design proponent and Scott Hartman who in addition to being a paleontologist is also an artist.
