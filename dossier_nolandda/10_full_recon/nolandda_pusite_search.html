Remember to use Proximity Operators
in Search Engines that support them:
  • ADJ -- adjacent words in any order
  • ADJ/# -- exactly # words apart in any order
  • OADJ -- adjacent words in order
  • OADJ/# -- exactly # words apart in order
  • NEAR -- within 25 words in any order
  • NEAR/# -- within # words in any order
  • ONEAR -- within 25 words in order
  • ONEAR/# -- within # words in order
  • FAR -- more than 25 words appart in any order
  • FAR/# -- more than # words appart in any order
  • OFAR -- more than 25 words appart in order
  • OFAR/# -- more than # words appart in order
