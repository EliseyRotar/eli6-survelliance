Pictures of Little Things         

Part of my research involves taking pictures of very tiny little things with a device called an atomic force microscope.
This page is a portfolio of some of my more aesthetically pleasing images.

An E. coli  bacterium.

A sheep's red blood cell. Don't ask me how I managed to acquire sheep blood. Let's just say Passover celebrations can get out of hand sometimes.

And here is my humble contribution to the advance of civilization: the world's smallest profanity (as far as I know). The letters are 100 nm high.

 

Bacillus anthracis bacterium hatching from a spore. Yes, they let idiots like me play with live anthrax strains. It's a scary world we live in.

This is a rare type of Chromium-resistant bacteria that was found in a toxic waste dump in Indiana.
