CamGuessr: live-camera guessing game

CamGuessr is a free GeoGuessr-style game built into TrafficVision.Live. Watch a live traffic camera feed and guess its location on the map. Three game modes:

  • Classic. 5 rounds, pin your guess on a world map, scored by distance (up to 5,000 points per round).
  • Streak. Endless rounds, but score below 1,000 points and the game ends.
  • Country. 5 rounds, guess which country the camera is in (1,000 points for correct, 250 for neighbouring countries).

CamGuessr filters through TrafficVision.Live's 155,000+ cameras to find eligible live video feeds from 130+ countries, with each stream tested in real time before the round starts to ensure a working feed. Submit your score to the global leaderboard.

What's the difference between traffic cameras and street cameras?
They refer to the same kind of feed: live cameras showing road conditions. "Traffic cameras" usually emphasize highways and freeways. "Live street cameras" often imply urban intersections and surface roads. TrafficVision.Live aggregates both highway-level and street-level live feeds in one map.
Can I watch live highway cameras from any U.S. state?
Yes. Every U.S. state DOT camera network is on TrafficVision.Live, including Caltrans, TxDOT, FDOT, GDOT, NYSDOT, CDOT, MDOT, ODOT, PennDOT, WSDOT, and 40+ others. Use the state filter or open the state guides for a full camera list per state.
Does TrafficVision.Live include 511 cameras?
Yes. TrafficVision.Live unifies all 50 state 511 systems, including 511NY, 511FL, 511CA, 511GA, 511PA, 511MN, and 45 others, into one searchable live map.
What's the best way to monitor traffic for a daily commute?
Use the route builder. Enter your start and destination, and TrafficVision.Live lists every live traffic camera along your drive. Save the route to check it daily, and bookmark frequently-watched cameras as favorites for one-tap access.
What's the AI overlay on TrafficVision.Live video feeds?
TrafficVision.Live runs computer vision on live video cameras to draw bounding boxes around vehicles and other objects, show real-time vehicle counts on screen, and provide automatic traffic detection. The overlays appear directly on the video feed.
