Where I am:
 
West Lafayette, Indiana, right smack-dab in the middle of the Heartland of America. And as it turns out, the heartland of this nation is clogged with trashy girls, cheap beer, and analytical chemistry. Go figure.
    
Why I'm here:
 
For as long as I can remember, I've always known that I wanted to pursue the sciences in some form or another. You know, I'm still not sure why I wound up doing chemistry. About halfway through my undergraduate career, I decided to go into analytical work. A summer working for NIH clinched the deal. So, off to Purdue University, the best place in the world for what I want to do (I still get a kick out of telling myself that).
 
The rent:

I currently teach second semester general chemistry.  I think I'm a pretty bad TA, though my students seem to like me. Here's the problem: when I was an undergrad I skipped all my recitation  sections, and now I'm not exactly sure what I'm supposed to do for 50 minutes a week. Basically, I answer homework problems and crack jokes.   
 
My most significant discovery to date:
 
I've found out where "The Village" from The Prisoner is. It's in Wales, England. If you have no idea what I'm talking about here, don't worry.
