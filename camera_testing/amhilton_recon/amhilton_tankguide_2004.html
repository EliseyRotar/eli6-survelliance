Field Guide to the Fish Tank:

Furious D   

Red Devil Cichlid (Astronatus ocellatus)

Favorite Food: Cichlid sticks, smaller fish.
Favorite Location: In the PVC pipe.
Likes: Chasing, eating other fish, hanging out in his pipe.
Dislikes: Everyone and everything.
Name Origin: Named after a character from "The Simpsons."

Notes: Being nocturnal, Furious D is far more fun to watch at night. Check out the webcam at around 1 AM EST to watch him chase the other fish around. Furious is highly territorial, and will eat any fish who isn't quick enough to get out of the way or covered with spines.

Captain Sucko   

Plecostomus (Hypostomus plecostomus)

Favorite Food: Best not to dwell on this.
Favorite Location: Attached to the glass.
Likes: Sucking.
Dislikes: Furious D.
Name Origin: Named after his favorite pastime.

Notes: Captain Sucko performs the important function of sucking algae off the sides of the tank. Furious D generally leaves Sucko alone, due to Sucko's protective covering of sharp spines.     

The Chairman   

Koi (Cyprinus carpio)

Favorite Food: Butterscotch-mango biscotti, cichlid sticks, hot dog bits.
Favorite Location: Far from Furious D.
Likes: Eating, swimmin' around.
Dislikes: Furious D.
Name Origin: Named after Chairman Kaga from "Iron Chef." Or maybe Chairman Mao, I'm not sure.

Notes: Like most Koi, The Chairman is growing at an impressive rate. As a consequence, The Chairman has a voracious appetite.

Whatshisname

Goldfish (Carassius auratus)

Favorite Food: Cichlid sticks, oatmeal.
Favorite Location: Far from Furious D.
Likes: Eating, Hanging out with Stumpy.
Dislikes: Furious D.
Name Origin: Named after out collective lack of imagination.

Notes: Some time ago, Brain bought 11 small goldfish. Within a few weeks, Furious managed to eat all but two of them. Since that time, these two goldfish have managed to grow to become too large for Furious D to eat, although Furious still enjoys chasing them around the tank. The goldifsh are both recovering nicely from septicemia, an infection of the blood.  

Stumpy

Goldfish (Carassius auratus)

Favorite Food: Cichlid sticks, oatmeal.
Favorite Location: Where Furious D is not.
Likes: Hiding in the stump, eating, hanging out with Whatshisname.
Dislikes: Furious D.
Name Origin: Named after the hollow stump in which this fish enjoys hiding.

Notes: Being the smallest of the fish, Stumpy has adopted the defensive tactic of hiding out in the hollow tree stump. Stumpy's been known to spend all afternoon hiding there.
