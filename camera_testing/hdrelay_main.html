Since 1998, we've helped customers deploy professional live camera systems for construction sites, commercial properties, golf courses, ski resorts, tourism, industrial facilities, public safety, and critical infrastructure.

Our complete camera platform combines rugged hardware, secure cloud services, and proprietary streaming technology to deliver reliable live video from virtually any location. Whether you need a single camera or a nationwide deployment, HD Relay provides everything needed to capture, stream, record, and manage your cameras remotely.

CONSTRUCTION CONTINGENCY PLANNING WITH REMOTE JOBSITE CAMERAS

Every construction project faces unexpected challenges, from severe weather and equipment failures to labor shortages, security incidents, and project delays. A well-developed construction contingency plan helps project teams respond quickly, minimize disruptions, and keep projects moving forward.

HD Relay construction cameras provide real-time visibility that supports contingency planning before, during, and after unexpected events. With live video, cloud recording, LTE connectivity, and remote access, project managers can quickly assess site conditions, coordinate response efforts, and make informed decisions without waiting for an onsite inspection.
