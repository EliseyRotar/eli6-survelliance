## What it was

The "Furious D Memorial Camera" was a **fish-tank webcam** in Al Hilton's Purdue University chemistry lab office.

### Al Hilton (the owner)

- **Username:** amhilton = Albert M. Hilton
- **Email:** particleman@purdue.edu
- **Affiliation:** Purdue University, Department of Chemistry
- **Lab:** Garth Simpson Group (analytical chemistry)
- **Role:** PhD student, 2nd-semester gen-chem TA (circa 2002-2008)
- **Site title:** "Al's Incredible Useless Webpage"

### The cam

- Two webcams: /furiousd/webcam.jpg (640x480) and /tank/tank.jpg
- Original cam source: 209.216.249.102/~simpson/webcam.jpg (Purdue Chemistry Simpson Group server)
- Page author tool: CoffeeCup WebCam 15000
- Refresh: every 5 seconds
- Cam **died ~2005** when Furious D (Red Devil Cichlid) died from "liver disease or pH imbalance"
- Image **froze** at 16,010 bytes (SHA1: 9df1250d7cb94e3c) from 2006 through at least 2014
- Server returned 404 for main and furiousd by 2014
